Pre-Election Expert Report

Who Will Win Punjab 2027?

Based on our comprehensive ground survey of over 50,000 respondents across 117 constituencies, here is our exclusive early prediction.

  • Aam Aadmi Party (AAP): Retains strong support in Malwa region, but faces anti-incumbency in urban pockets.
  • Indian National Congress (INC): Seeing a resurgence in Majha, focusing heavily on local leadership.
  • Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD): Attempting a massive comeback by reconnecting with the rural vote bank.
  • Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP): Going solo, aiming to consolidate urban voters and expand its footprint.
