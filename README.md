# India Voter 🇮🇳
> **The Pulse of the Nation** — Ground Reality Reports, Real Election Field Surveys, and Pre-Election Expert Analysis.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**India Voter** is a dedicated election ground intelligence and voter sentiment portal led by chief ground analyst & founder **Vijay Godara**. Unlike traditional studio pundits, India Voter conducts on-the-spot voter interviews, maintains handwritten booth-level field registers, and broadcasts real-time sentiment directly from the grassroots.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 📸 Ground Reality: Live On-Site Survey Operations
- **Real Field Photos**: Authentic survey photos from high-stakes assembly and municipal campaigns across India.
  - **Bihar (Bankipur, Patna)**: Exclusive interaction & pre-poll mandate calculation with Jan Suraaj party founder **Prashant Kishor (P.K.)**.
  - **Delhi (Uttam Nagar & Delhi NCT)**: Door-to-door sampling with active handwritten voter registers and analysis of welfare guarantees (Auto/Taxi/E-Rickshaw ₹10 Lakh insurance scheme).
  - **Haryana (Uklana Mandi, Hisar & Sirsa)**: Ward-by-ward municipal survey in official India Voter kit.
  - **Rajasthan (Bhadra & Mangrol)**: Grassroots interviews with the branded India Voter microphone outside Govt Primary School No. 3 (Bhadra, Hanumangarh) and commercial trader sentiment in Mangrol (Baran).
- **Interactive State Filters**: Seamless filtering by `Delhi`, `Bihar`, `Haryana`, `Rajasthan`, or `All Surveys`.
- **Accessible Lightbox Modal**: Native `<dialog>` modal showing full-resolution photos, field notes, and direct verified Facebook links.

### 2. 🎥 Exclusive Field Video Reports & Reels Hub
- **Embedded Facebook Reels Player**: Responsive, live iframe embed streaming high-engagement field reels.
- **Interactive Playlist**: Switch effortlessly between:
  - *Uklana Nagar Palika Election Calculation* (**7.4K+ Views · 331 Reactions**)
  - *Bankipur Patna Bihar Ground Mic Survey* (Street interviews with India Voter mic)
  - *Kolkata Bhabanipur Ground Survey* (**4.9K+ Views · 253 Reactions**)
  - *Sirsa Sahri Chunav Anuman* (**1.3K+ Views · 127 Reactions**)
  - *Bhabanipur Kolkata Rally Coverage* (**1.1K+ Views · 80 Reactions**)
- **Theater Mode View**: Fullscreen dialog player with direct Facebook verification buttons.
